How to Convert MPG to L/100KM?

Conversion Factors:

  • 1 mile = 1.609344 kilometers
  • 1 US gallon = 3.78541178 liters

Formula Derivation:

  • Start with: MPG = Miles ÷ Gallon
  • Convert miles: 1.609344 km ÷ Gallon
  • Convert gallon: 1.609344 km ÷ 3.78541178 liters
  • Simplify: 2.35214583 liters/km
  • For 100km: 235.214583 liters/100km

Final Formula:

L/100KM = 235.214583 ÷ MPG

❓MPG (US) to L/100km Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is MPG (US)?

MPG (US) stands for Miles Per Gallon (US). It shows how many miles a vehicle can travel using one US gallon of fuel.

QWhat is L/100km?

L/100km stands for Liters Per 100 Kilometers. It shows how many liters of fuel a vehicle uses to travel 100 kilometers.

QHow do I convert MPG (US) to L/100km?

Use the following formula: L/100km = 235.214583 ÷ MPG (US)

QWhat is considered a good MPG (US)?

For most vehicles, 25–30 MPG is considered average. A fuel economy of 40 MPG or higher is generally considered excellent.

QWhy do many countries use L/100km instead of MPG?

Most countries use the metric system, so fuel consumption is measured in Liters Per 100 Kilometers (L/100km) instead of miles per gallon.

QIs this MPG (US) to L/100km converter accurate?

Yes. This converter uses the official conversion factor of 235.214583, providing accurate and reliable results.

QWhat is the difference between MPG (US) and MPG (UK)?

A US gallon equals 3.785 liters, while a UK (Imperial) gallon equals 4.546 liters. Because the UK gallon is larger, the same vehicle will have a higher MPG (UK) value than MPG (US).

QDoes a lower L/100km value mean better fuel efficiency?

Yes. A lower L/100km value means the vehicle uses less fuel to travel 100 kilometers, resulting in better fuel efficiency and lower fuel costs.

QCan I use this converter for petrol, diesel, and hybrid vehicles?

Yes. This converter works for petrol, diesel, hybrid, and other fuel-powered vehicles because it converts measurement units, not fuel types.

QCan I enter decimal values in this converter?

Yes. You can enter both whole numbers and decimal values. The converter instantly calculates the result using your selected decimal precision.
